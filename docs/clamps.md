# Clamps

For an appliance or duct whose size you know only roughly, or one you may swap for another of a
similar size: an opening in one piece, and four printed jaws that slide in on bolts and grip it.
Where you can measure exactly and want the opening sealed, the measured cutout, with its rim
lined by the seal, is the better choice.

## What you get

- **The piece**: an opening the appliance's measured size plus half the jaws' travel each side,
  and eight 4.5 mm bolt holes round it, 6 mm out from the opening. Nothing stands out of it, so it
  prints flat like any other piece.
- **Four jaws**, printed beside the pieces: two long ones for the top and bottom, two short ones
  for the sides. Each is a flat foot, as thick as the panel, with two slots in it, and a lip along
  one edge that presses against the appliance. They print flat, foot down, with no supports.
- **The plan's line** names the opening, the sizes the jaws can grip, and the bolts to use.

With a travel of 10 mm (the starting value, under **Advanced: the clamps**), a 120 × 80 mm
appliance gets a 130 × 90 mm opening, and the jaws grip anything from 110 × 70 to 130 × 90 mm.

## Fitting it

You need eight M4 bolts, each with a nut and two washers. The plan gives their least length:
twice the panel's thickness and 6 mm, 12 mm for a 3 mm panel.

1. Put the wall in the window and pass the appliance through the opening.
2. On the room side, lay each jaw on the panel with its lip against the appliance and its slots
   over the bolt holes: the long jaws at the top and bottom, the short ones at the sides.
3. Push a bolt through each slot and its hole, with a washer under the head and another under
   the nut, and tighten it finger tight.
4. Slide each jaw in until its lip presses on the appliance, then tighten its two bolts.

## The corners

The long jaws cover the opening's corners; the short ones fit between them when the jaws are
slid in as far as they go. So unless the appliance is at the smallest size the jaws grip, a
small gap stays open at each corner of the opening, between a short jaw's end and a long jaw: at
most the travel by the travel and 0.5 mm (10 × 10.5 mm at the start), and about 5 × 5.5 mm near
the measured size. Stuff it with foam tape or anything soft if air must not pass. The jaws are not
lined with the seal.
