# Foam tape sizes

The tape seal (backlog item `seal-tape`, #15) lists four sizes of self-adhesive foam
weather-seal tape, starting at the first (Q121). Any other size can be typed in. Sizes come
from the makers' own product pages, read on 5 Oct 2026; sizes a maker gives in inches are
converted at 25.4 mm per inch and rounded to 0.1 mm.

## Listed sizes

| Width (mm) | Thickness (mm) | Makers' products of that size                                                    | Gap the maker states (mm)              |
| ---------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 9          | 4              | tesa PE-Schaumstoff S (T1); Schellenberg Schaumstoffdichtung Plus, 4.5 thick (S1) | 1 to 3.5 (tesa); 1 to 4.5 (Schellenberg) |
| 9.5        | 4.8            | M-D Foam Window Seal and Economy Foam (M1, M2); Frost King Vinyl Foam and Rubber Foam (F1, F2); Duck Foam Weatherstrip, small (D1) | 1.6 to 4.8 (M-D)                       |
| 9          | 6              | tesa Schaumstoff M (T2); Ellen Tochtband Schuim (E1)                              | 1 to 5 (tesa, Ellen)                   |
| 9.5        | 6.4            | Frost King Vinyl Foam and Poly Foam (F1, F3); Duck Heavy-Duty, 9.7 wide (D2)      | Not stated                             |

Across the six makers read, a width of 9 to 9.5 mm is sold by all six; 4 to 5 mm thick and 6 to
6.4 mm thick by five each. The largest gap each maker states is 80 to 100 % of the tape's
thickness and the smallest 17 to 33 %, so a tape squeezed to half its thickness, as the panels
are sized for (Q125), is inside every stated range.

## How the panels use it

- The groove is 1 mm deep to start with (Q124) and the tape's width plus 0.5 mm wide, so a 9 mm
  and a 9.5 mm tape both fit one groove.
- Squeezed to half its thickness (Q125), a 4 mm tape in a 1 mm groove stands 1 mm out of it.
  That is the room the panels leave for it.
- The same tape goes in every place chosen (Q130): the edge strips' outer face, and under
  Advanced their sides (Q127) and the inside of the hose port's collar (Q129). The joints
  between pieces are left to the printed TPU seal (Q128).
