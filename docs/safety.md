# Before you fit it

A wall in a window sits near children, escape routes, heaters and power cords, and in winter it
can run wet. The page says the same things as this file, in a section of its own. Where each comes
from is in the design review (`research/design-review.md`).

## Read this first

- The wall is not a fall barrier: it will not stop a child, or anyone, falling out of the window.
  About 5,200 children a year are treated in the United States after a fall from a window, and
  screens do not prevent one (verified in the review).
- Do not block a bedroom’s only escape window unless the wall comes out in seconds, by hand, with
  no tool. A bedroom's escape opening must open from inside without a key, a tool or special
  knowledge (verified in the review, from the building code). The sash stop
  ([`sash-stop.md`](sash-stop.md)) lifts out by hand for this reason.
- On a ground floor, or wherever the window can be reached from outside, add a metal window lock:
  neither the wall nor its sash stop is a lock.
- In winter, check the wall and the window for condensation, and air the room if they run wet. A
  single printed wall's room side reaches the dew point at about 40 % room humidity with 0 °C
  outside; a double wall raises that ([`double-wall.md`](double-wall.md)).
- PLA softens in a sunny window: print the wall in PETG, or in ASA where it gets sun. Why:
  [`materials.md`](materials.md).
- Printed plastics burn: keep the wall away from heaters and flames, and do not trap a power cord in
  it. Printed plastics are at best in the slow-burning class unless sold as flame retardant
  (verified in the review).
- Put the wall together whole, away from the window, then set it in the window in one piece.

## What is not claimed

This project claims no energy saving, and the wall is not airtight, weatherproof, secure, child-safe or
flame retardant; it has no wind, rain or UV rating.

What it does do is worked out or relayed in each reader's file, with how sure each figure is. None
of it has been measured on a wall in a window yet.
