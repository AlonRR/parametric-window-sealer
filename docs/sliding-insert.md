# Sliding insert

For an appliance or duct you can measure, or one that may be swapped for another of a similar
width: an opening in one piece, and two shutters that slide in from each side and close round it.
Unlike the clamps, it leaves nothing open but a small clearance above and below the appliance.

## What you get

- **The piece**: an opening as tall as the appliance with 1 mm to spare above and below, and as wide
  as the appliance plus the shutters' travel; a bolt hole 6 mm beyond each side of it; and two rails
  standing on its room face above and below the opening, each with a dovetail groove facing it. The
  rails' 45° slopes print without supports, the piece lying flat as usual.
- **Two shutters**, printed beside the pieces: flat plates 2.4 mm thick, their long edges bevelled to
  slide in the grooves, each with a slot for its bolt.
- **The plan's line** names the opening, the widths the shutters close round, and the bolt to use.

With a travel of 20 mm (the starting value, under **Advanced: the sliding insert**), a
120 × 80 mm appliance gets a 140 × 82 mm opening, and the shutters close round anything from 100 to
140 mm wide.

## Fitting it

You need two M4 bolts, each with a nut and two washers. The plan gives their least length: the
panel's thickness and 8.4 mm, to the next whole mm (12 mm for a 3 mm panel).

1. Put the wall in the window and pass the appliance through the opening.
2. On the room side, slide a shutter into the rails from each end, its slot over the bolt hole.
3. Push a bolt through each slot and its hole, with a washer under the head and the nut.
4. Slide each shutter in until it meets the appliance, then tighten its bolt.
