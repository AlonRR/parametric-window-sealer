# Printing the pieces

A large flat print lifts first at its square corners as it cools, and its first layers spread a
little where they meet the bed. So every printed piece has:

- **Rounded corners**, 3 mm round. Where four pieces meet, the corners sit inside the cross piece,
  and at the wall's corners inside the edge strips, so the rounding does not show.
- **A 1 mm chamfer** along its bed-side edge, the outside face as it prints, at 45°. It sits in the
  strips' slots, and takes up the first layers' spread.

Whatever stands on a piece's room side, a hose port's collar or a cutout's rim, is kept as it is.
Pieces cut from sheet are cut square. The preview draws every piece square, as the rounding is too
small to see there; the files have it.

## Brims

The plan gives advice for the material chosen (inferred, from common printing guides; the design
review found no measured source for it):

| Material | Brim |
| --- | --- |
| PLA | Seldom needed |
| PETG | 5 mm, if the corners lift |
| ASA | 5 mm, and print in an enclosure or out of draughts: it warps as it cools |

Nothing is added to the files for a brim; set it in your slicer.
