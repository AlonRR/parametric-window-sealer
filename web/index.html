Parametric window sealer

Printable panels that close off the part of a window left open around an air conditioner, a fan or a hose.

The opening

Example sizes: measure the opening you can see in your own window. Enter the depth of its tracks under The window.

The window

Sets what each edge of the wall does. Change any edge below if your window differs. Each edge gets an edge strip: in a track it fills the track; against the sash or on a sill it gives a flat face.

Example sizes: measure your own tracks. Every edge strip is this wide.

What is in the window?

The piece the port is centred in, counted from 1 at the bottom left, seen from the room. It starts in the bottom row's middle piece.

Seal

Self-adhesive foam weather-seal tape. It goes in a groove along each edge strip's outer face, where the track, the sash or the sill presses on it.
